Variable Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Data stored on your device

Your saved games, statistics, settings and purchases are stored locally on your device. We do not collect this data and it is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded ads, shown only when you choose to watch one to earn an extra hint or check. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and, where you consent, to personalize them. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, you are asked for consent before ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, then Privacy & ad choices. On iOS you can also manage tracking permission in your device settings. You can use the app without watching any ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
